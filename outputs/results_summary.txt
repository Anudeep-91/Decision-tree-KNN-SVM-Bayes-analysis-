Architectural & Algorithmic Observations:
  1. Support Vector Machine (Linear SVM):
     - Typically achieves superior precision and recall in high-dimensional text spaces.
     - The linear hyperplane effectively separates sparse TF-IDF vectors where data points
       are naturally linearly separable due to high dimensionality.
  2. Multinomial Naïve Bayes:
     - Delivers near-peak performance with near-zero training latency.
     - Extremely resilient to irrelevant vocabulary features due to Laplace smoothing.
  3. Decision Tree:
     - Suffers from greedy orthogonal splits on sparse feature representations.
     - Highly prone to variance and overfitting without depth constraints or ensemble averaging.
  4. K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN):
     - Severely affected by the 'Curse of Dimensionality' in 10,000-dimensional TF-IDF space,
       where Euclidean/Minkowski distances between sparse vectors concentrate into uniform distances.
